# 2026-09-13 — Hard edges

Seed: Robert Indiana, the hard-edge pop painter, was born on this date. A six-colour
e-paper panel is a hard-edge painting machine, so today is five kinds of hard edge.

## 1. First Light, Blue Water
Hanga-style stacked wave rows, blue in charge, white crests, a red sun just clearing the
horizon and one black boat. Python/PIL, palette-exact, no dither. Morning because it's quiet
and the sun is still low.

## 2. Thirteen
Pop signage: concentric rings, stencil numerals cut by a bar, two green stars breaking the
circle, a printed rule top and bottom. Yellow dominant. Hand-written SVG. Midday because it's
loud, and the sun is on the frame.

## 3. Two O'Clock Table
A 40-line numpy raytracer: four spheres on a checked floor under hard afternoon light with long
black shadows, then Floyd–Steinberg dithered so the spheres carry a "printed halftone" look. The
one 3D piece of the day. Afternoon because that's when shadows get long.

## 4. Geese, Going South
Linocut mood: banded red sky, yellow band and low sun, black and green hills, a skein of geese
and a bare tree. Red dominant. SVG. Evening, mid-September — the geese are actually leaving.

## 5. Rule 30, Blue
An elementary cellular automaton grown from a single seed, 10px cells, blue on black, white
where three cells agree, a sliver of yellow moon. Night: mostly black, with a little light in it.

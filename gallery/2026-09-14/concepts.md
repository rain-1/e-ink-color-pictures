# 2026-09-14 — Stelle (restricted palettes)

Seed: Dante died in Ravenna on this day in 1321. Each of the three canticles of the Comedy
ends on the same word, *stelle*. So: five pieces from the poem, each restricted to two or
three of the six colours, each containing exactly one star.

## 1. Selva Oscura — black, green, white
Inferno I, the dark wood at dawn. A woodcut: white paper, black trunks in perspective with
knife-cut bark, green undergrowth of leaf-litter marks, and seven shafts of light cut out of
the ground from a sun that hasn't cleared the trees. The star is the morning star in a gap
in the canopy. Morning, because it's the moment of waking lost.

## 2. The Mountain — yellow, black, white
Purgatorio. Seven terraces stacked like a wedding cake, each with a shaded face and rock
strata on the lit side; a stair zig-zags up the sunward side past the gate; a small climber
with a staff is on the fifth terrace; a single tree on the summit under the star. A hard
white sun in a yellow sky. Midday, because Purgatory is climbed in daylight — by Dante's
rules you cannot climb at night.

## 3. Nine Circles — black, red only
Inferno as a 3D render. A raymarched heightfield of nine concentric terraces dropping
toward a shaft, seen from above the rim, lit low from the left so every wall throws a
shadow, with depth fog to black. All tone is carried by an 8×8 Bayer screen. The one
star is negative space: the shaft at the bottom is pure black. Afternoon — the descent.

## 4. The Spheres — blue, yellow, white
Paradiso as an astronomer's plate: Earth, then the spheres of the Moon, Mercury, Venus,
Sun, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, the fixed stars and the Primum Mobile, each ring engraved with
a doubled line, 72 minor ticks, 12 major, and its Latin name on the arc. The fixed-star
sphere is a band of 210 white pinpricks. The star is Venus, white, on her ring. Evening,
because Venus is the evening star.

## 5. Riveder le Stelle — black, white only
The last line of the Inferno: "and then we came out to see the stars again." A chart of
the real mid-September evening sky over Italy: Vega, Deneb and Altair with Lyra, Cygnus and
Aquila joined up, the Summer Triangle dashed, the Milky Way as stipple, a dotted RA/Dec
grid. At the foot, two small figures step up out of a black cleft. Night. Positions are from
memory and approximate; good enough at four inches.

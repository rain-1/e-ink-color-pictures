# 2026-09-19 — Colours

Seed: September 19 is International Talk Like a Pirate Day — read straight, not
in costume-shop kitsch, as a day of ships, charts, and knots. The autumn
equinox lands three days from now, so night leans into day and night drawing
close to even. A small flag or pennant appears in every piece — the day's
spine.

## 1. Pleasant Voyage
Hard-edge signal-flag poster, red/blue/white. The International Code hoist
"UW" — "I wish you a pleasant voyage" — run up the mast at first light,
gulls overhead, a low white sun. Morning is the day's send-off.

## 2. Soundings
A fathometer chart plate, green ink on black, dominant black. A jagged
headland along the top, a 32-point compass rose commanding the lower half,
depth soundings scattered through the open water, one pennant marking a
fixed buoy. Midday is the working chart, read while the sun is overhead.

## 3. The Loop
A technical rope diagram, blue ink on white, dominant white. A bowline's
eye with its tucked bight, hatched for roundness like a woodcut in two
colours, the working end flying a small pennant. Afternoon is hands-on —
a knot tied, not just drawn.

## 4. Sunset Colours
Cut-paper silhouette, red on yellow, dominant yellow. A boat's hull wide
against a setting sun, the ensign only part-way down the mast — the naval
ritual of striking the colours at day's end, mirroring the flags raised
this morning.

## 5. Dead Reckoning
An aquatint night chart: stippled stars on black, six marked and one ringed
for Polaris, a thin band of the day's last light at the horizon. A small
ship crosses under sail, a tiny pennant at the stern, steering by the stars
as day and night draw level before the equinox.

# 2026-09-20 — Armada de Molucca

Seed: on this day in 1519 five ships left Sanlúcar de Barrameda under Magellan; one came
back. Five ships, five slots, one voyage. The owner asked for much more detail than the
last run, so every piece here is drawn dense: hatching, rigging, place names, dimension
lines, star catalogues. All six colours are allowed again; each piece still has one in
charge. A nao appears somewhere in every one.

## 1. Sanlúcar, dawn
White in charge. An engraved harbour plate. The Guadalquivir mouth at sunrise: hatched sky, an engraved sun
with rings and rays, lens clouds with their own hatching, gulls, the town along the shore
(white houses, red roofs, the castle of Santiago, the church tower), stone pines on the
Doñana spit, and the five carracks heading out — each with hull wales, castles, three
masts, bellying square sails carrying the cross of Burgundy, lateen mizzen, shrouds,
ratlines, stays and red swallow-tailed flags. Morning, because they sailed at dawn.

## 2. Carta de marear
White in charge, yellow land. A portolan chart of the Atlantic with the fleet's track. Portolan grammar kept honestly:
rhumb lines in the three traditional inks (black for the eight winds, green for the
half-winds, red for the quarter-winds) from sixteen nodes, land in yellow with a blue
coast fringe, names written on the land side with the chief ports in red, a 32-point rose
with a fleur-de-lis, mountains, rivers, tents in the Sahara, a spouting whale, equator and
tropics, a trunk of leagues. The route is dotted; the fleet glyph shrinks along it — five,
five, four (Santiago wrecked at Santa Cruz, marked), three into the strait. Midday, a
navigator's noon.

## 3. Estrecho de Todos los Santos
White in charge, blue and green. A raymarched fjord, seen from the water. Ridged rock, forest at the shore, snow above,
mist with distance, and the whole scene reflected in the channel. Rendered as a
screenprint: each material is screened between two inks with an 8×8 Bayer matrix (rock
black/white, forest black/green, snow blue/white, water black/blue/white) so nothing
speckles. Three naos in the channel with their reflections. Afternoon, the long passage.

## 4. Nao Victoria, lámina IV
Blue in charge. A shipwright's blueprint of the one ship that came home: sheer plan with the full sail
plan (yellow sails, blue seams, white rigging), body plan of frames with waterlines and
buttocks, half-breadth deck plan with masts, hatches and capstan, a table of the sails, a
metre scale, dimension lines with arrowheads, a red load waterline. Dimensions are the
replica's; nobody kept the originals. Evening, the drawing-office hour.

## 5. Nubeculae
Black in charge. The southern sky as the fleet saw it from the strait, late October 1520, ten at night,
looking south from 53°S: star positions computed from a catalogue of ~200 stars and a
real sidereal time, so Achernar sits near the zenith, Canopus in the south-east, the Cross
low in the Milky Way. The Milky Way is sampled from the galactic plane with the Coalsack
cut out; the two Magellanic Clouds are stippled with the LMC's bar. Below, the last of
the twilight, the black shore of Tierra del Fuego with its campfires and their reflections
in the water, and the three ships at anchor with one lantern each. Night.

# 2026-09-30 — Die Zauberflöte, 235 years

Mozart's *Magic Flute* premiered on this night in 1791 at the Theater auf der Wieden in
Vienna, Mozart conducting, Schikaneder as Papageno. Five scenes from it, from dawn to the
Queen's night. The owner asked for much more detail and the full palette, so every piece
this time is dense, drawn, and uses as many colours as it wants.

## 1. Der Vogelfänger
Papageno's onion-domed cage hung from an autumn branch at sunrise over Vienna: ten songbirds
on three perches, the door swung open, one bird already out on it and two more flying for the
sun, the pan-pipes hung from the hook. Behind it the Stephansdom's zig-zag roof and spire, the
baroque domes, the Danube. Morning because the bird-catcher's song is a morning song.

## 2. O Isis und Osiris
Sarastro's temple as a painted hypostyle hall in one-point perspective: banded papyrus columns
with glyph panels, a blue ceiling of yellow stars, winged-sun cross-beams, the gate at the far
end and noon light throwing the column shadows toward us. Two tiny travellers (one carrying a
cage) walk the red runner. Midday: the realm of the sun.

## 3. Die Feuer- und Wasserprobe
A geological cutaway of a mountain in red strata. A cavern of fire at the root, a cavern of
water above it, ladders between, the lovers on a bridge over the flames while the flute's notes
rise through the rock toward the temple and the sun on the summit. Afternoon: the day's trial.

## 4. Wie stark ist nicht dein Zauberton
Tamino plays and the beasts come, as a millefleur tapestry: a green ground scattered with
daisies, pinks, bluebells, strawberries and ferns; a lion, a stag, a fox, three hares, five
birds and a snail gathered round an orange tree; the evening star and the new moon woven in
above, and a vine border. Evening: the soft, gathering hour.

## 5. Der Hölle Rache
After Schinkel's 1815 set for the Queen of the Night: a vault of stars in perspective rows, the
Queen with a glory of rays and a star-strewn cloak standing on a crescent above a bank of cloud,
framed by a proscenium, with footlights and the aria's high staccato arpeggio on a staff below.
Night: it's her hour, and the vault stays mostly dark.

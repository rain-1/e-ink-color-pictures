# 2026-10-02 — Rocket Sunday (full palette, full detail)

Seed: two birthdays on 2 October. Alex Raymond (b. 1909), who drew *Flash Gordon* with
a cinematic dry-brush finish, and Gwen Marston (b. 1936), who taught "liberated
quiltmaking" — no patterns, no rulers. The Spectra-6 panel is nearly the ink set of a
1930s Sunday colour press, so today uses all six, everywhere, with Ben-Day dots and
screens for tone. A finned red rocket appears in every piece.

## 1. Dawn Launch
A deco poster: screened sky ramp (blue → white → yellow → red) with ray wedges, a
striped sun, two depths of towers with hundreds of lit windows, skyway arches, a lattice
gantry, a riveted rocket on a column of Ben-Day-shaded smoke, and watchers on a balcony.
Morning, because it's lift-off.

## 2. Rocket Sunday
An original five-panel Sunday page: masthead, a ringed red planet, a pilot facing an alien
sunrise, a ZZAP! burst, a landing among giant fungi, and a "next week" box. Black key
line, flat inks, dots for every tone. Midday, when the funnies were read.

## 3. Liberated Medallion
A quilt for Gwen Marston: wonky liberated stars across the top, flying-geese columns going
opposite ways, a sawtooth ring around a pieced rocket and an appliquéd planet, and eight
wonky log cabins at the foot. Every patch is a printed fabric (dots, calico, gingham,
stripes, check); big-stitch hand quilting changes thread colour with the ground. Afternoon:
the slow, warm, hand-made hour.

## 4. Jungle Moonrise
Dusk on Mongo: a huge cratered moon over red cloud strata, a far blue jungle with a
moon-side rim light, a river reflecting it all, green bottle trees and pitcher plants, and
recursive black fern fronds with glowing pods. A small explorer with a lantern. Evening,
because the light is going.

## 5. Night Watch
Inside the rocket, lights down. A deco-arched windscreen on the Milky Way, tonight's
waning gibbous moon (lit from the left) and an early Draconid; below, dials, a radar
scope, bar meters, toggles with tell-tale lamps, throttles, and a postcard of this
morning's launch. 81% black: the night slot.

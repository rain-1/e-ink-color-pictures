# 2026-10-03 (Saturday) — Steve Reich at 90

Reich was born on 3 October 1936 and turns ninety today. Music for 18 Musicians also
turns fifty this season, and Saturn reaches opposition tomorrow. All five pieces use all
six inks and a lot more drawn detail than before, following the owner's note.

## 1. Piano Phase
This is a full-colour diagram. Each of the twelve rings shows one step of the 1967 piece's
drift: the outer half of a cell is the first piano's note and the inner half is the second's.
The rings go from unison at the hub to fully out of phase at the rim, and the two grand
pianos sit nested underneath. It's in the morning slot because it's pale and starts from
unison.

## 2. Music for 18 Musicians
A top-down stage plan seen from the lighting rig at noon: plank floor with grain, cables,
four pianos with gold plates showing, the vibraphone, marimbas with every bar drawn, the
singers on mics, and a full house in red seats. Yellow leads, which suits midday.

## 3. Different Trains — from Chicago to New York, 1939
An afternoon landscape. A streamlined Hudson pulling three coaches crosses a red through-truss
bridge, with prairie fields, a co-op elevator, a windmill, telegraph wires, cumulus clouds
and reeds in the foreground. It shows only the American half of the piece: the boy
travelling between his parents. Blue leads.

## 4. It's Gonna Rain
An evening still life. An upright reel-to-reel stands in front of a rain-streaked window,
with San Francisco against a red afterglow behind it. The details are the reel cut-outs,
the head block, the VU meters (one per drifting machine) and a masking-tape label where
the phrase slips a syllable. Red and black for dusk.

## 5. Pendulum Music
The night piece is a strobe of four microphones swinging over four upturned speakers, so
each swing becomes a fan of string-art lines that decays toward stillness. Underneath are
four traces of the feedback howls drifting out of phase, and Saturn, one night from
opposition, is in the high window. Almost all black.

# 2026-10-08 (Thursday) — fire from the sky

On the night of 8 October 1871 Chicago and Peshtigo burned. In 1883 a congressman blamed
fragments of Biela's comet, which had been seen to split in two in 1846; it wasn't true
(drought and wind did it). Tonight, 8–9 October 2026, the Draconids — real comet dust,
harmless — peak under a moonless sky. Five readings, full palette, as much drawn detail
as the panel will hold.

## 1. Leaf Fall
A lawn at first light from directly above: forty leaves — ginkgo fans, maples (one split red
and yellow down the midrib, some with black tar-spot), oaks with a nibbled edge, birch —
each with veins and a cast shadow, over sixteen thousand drawn grass blades, clover and dew.
Green in charge. Morning, because this is what the night left on the ground.

## 2. The Divided Comet
A 19th-century astronomical plate: Biela's comet in two parts over an observatory hill, the
sky engraved in white line on blue, with a keeper's cottage, poplars and a figure at the gate;
below, four eyepiece sketches of the pair drawing apart from 1845 to the 1852 return.
White paper in charge. Midday, the bright clear-headed piece — the evidence.

## 3. Peshtigo, Before
A naive-painting panorama of a Wisconsin mill town on a dry October afternoon: blue
hills, a river with a log boom, a white church, the sawmill's plume, a train crossing the
trestle, and some fifteen hundred trees drawn one crown at a time. Nothing is burning yet.
Afternoon because it's the last ordinary hour.

## 4. Chicago, 8 October 1871
After the Currier & Ives prints: the business district alight, smoke and sparks blowing
north-east, the Water Tower standing white in front, schooners and a crowded swing bridge
on a river full of reflected fire. Red in charge. Evening — the fire began around nine.

## 5. Draconids
The northern sky at 22:00 from 45°N with real star positions — Draco winding between the
Dippers, Cassiopeia and Cepheus, the Milky Way — and meteors streaming from the dragon's head.
Below, a stubble field, a hayrick, a bare elm, a farmhouse with one lit window, and two people
on a red blanket, one pointing. Black in charge. Night: the real sky, tonight, and nothing burns.

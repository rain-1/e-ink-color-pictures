# 2026-10-09 — Hangul Day (full palette, full detail)

Seed: 9 October is Hangul Day, the anniversary of the 1446 publication of the
Hunminjeongeum, King Sejong's alphabet. Its consonants are drawn from the shapes of the
mouth, and its vowels from three strokes: · heaven, ㅡ earth, ㅣ person. Last night was also
the Draconid peak, with no moon (new moon tomorrow). Today I used all six colours and
much more drawn detail, as the owner asked.

## 1. Sun, Moon and Five Peaks
After the Irworobongdo screens that stood behind Joseon thrones: a red sun and a white moon,
five peaks built from nested contour bands, two white waterfalls, red-trunked pines with
scaled bark and needle fans, and a sea of fish-scale waves. Heaven, earth and person in
landscape form. It goes in the morning slot because the sun and moon are both up at dawn.

## 2. The Correct Sounds
The Haerye as an anatomical plate: a head in hatched section with the tongue in red and
leader lines to ㄱ ㅁ ㄴ ㅅ ㅇ, each letter drawn from the organ that makes it. Below that
is the add-a-stroke table (ㄱ→ㅋ, ㄴ→ㄷ→ㅌ …) and the three vowel roots. The letters are
drawn as strokes in code because there's no Korean font installed, which fits an alphabet
designed from geometry. It's a midday piece because it's the one to read up close.

## 3. Chaekgeori
A scholar's bookshelf screen with twelve compartments: tied books with ruled page edges, a
celadon vase of chrysanthemums, a dish of persimmons, brushes and an inkstone, scrolls, red
coral, spectacles, a pocket watch, peacock feathers. Yellow wood with drawn grain and brass
corners, set against papered back walls. It's an afternoon piece for slow looking.

## 4. Magpie's Rice
*Kkachi-bap*: the persimmons a farmer leaves on the tree for the magpies. There's a
recursive black tree, about fifty Bayer-screened persimmons, two magpies, and a dusk sky
going from blue through red to yellow, with the sun behind cloud bars. Below are a stubble
field, a tiled stone wall and the red-and-green rafter ends of a hanok eave. Evening.

## 5. Draconids
The night after the shower's peak, with no moon. Draco winds across the top with meteors
fanning out from its head, the Little Dipper and Polaris sit above it, and the Milky Way
is a sparse stipple with a dark rift. Below are misted ridges and a village with three lit
paper-lattice windows, plus one person lying on a roof pointing up. About 95% black. Star
positions are stylised.

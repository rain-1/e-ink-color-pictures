# 2026-10-12 — Behold, the sea itself (full palette, full detail)

Seed: two things that happened on 12 October. In 1492 the lookout on the Pinta saw land and
Columbus came ashore at Guanahaní. In 1910 Vaughan Williams conducted the first performance of
*A Sea Symphony* at Leeds on his 38th birthday. It opens with the chorus singing Whitman,
"Behold, the sea itself!" So the day is a day of sea. The owner asked for much more detail and said
restricted palettes were only an occasional thing, so every piece here uses all six colours and
packs in as much drawn detail as the panel can hold.

## 1. Tierra!
Woodblock print, all six colours. It is dawn on 12 October 1492. The Pinta is under full sail,
with planked hull, castles, rigging, Castile pennants and red-cross sails. A lookout in the
crow's nest points at a palm-fringed island, and the sun comes up behind it. The sky is cut
into bands from night blue down to red, the sea is rows of Hokusai wave marks, and Venus is the
last star still out. Morning, because it is the moment of first sight.

## 2. Flaunt Out, O Sea
Harbour poster with a key plate. A barque lies at anchor dressed overall. The International
Code flags on her dressing lines spell out FLAUNT OUT O SEA BEHOLD THE SEA ITSELF. The picture
has furled sails, ratlines, a lighthouse and a little painted town. Below it is a key to all
26 flags with Whitman's line and the Leeds premiere date. The Code flags use exactly the
panel's palette, which is why this piece exists. Midday, for bright bunting.

## 3. Carta de la Mar Océana
A portolan chart of the first voyage, laid out sideways (east up, north to the left) the way
portolans were read from any side of the table. It has a network of 32 rhumb lines from a
central rose and sixteen satellite roses, with winds in black, green and red. It also has a
fleur-de-lis north point, coloured islands, the Sargasso weed, a sea creature, three caravels,
dated stations along the route (Palos, Gomera, the turn after the birds on 7 Oct) and a scale of
leagues. Parchment yellow is in charge. Afternoon, for slow reading.

## 4. The Sea Itself
A 3D render: analytic wave normals on a plane, Fresnel reflection of a banded sunset sky, and a
specular sun road. Each pixel's tone runs along one ramp, black→blue→red→yellow→white, and is
screened with an 8×8 Bayer matrix. The three ships sit as silhouettes on the horizon. Evening,
for the sun going down on the last full day before landfall.

## 5. Candelilla
10 p.m. on 11 October 1492, on the Santa María's quarterdeck. The Admiral thinks he sees a light
to the west, "like a little wax candle, rising and falling", and calls Pedro Gutiérrez to look.
Two figures stand at the rail, rim-lit by the stern lantern, under a stippled Milky Way with
its dark rift. The rigging is dark blue against the stars, and there is one yellow pinprick on
the horizon, four hours before land. Night, and almost all black.

# 2026-11-24 — the Beaver Moon

Seed: tonight's full moon is the Beaver Moon, and a perigee supermoon (full at 14:53 UT,
sitting between the Pleiades and Aldebaran, with Uranus at opposition next door). Five
readings of it, following the animal the moon is named for. It is also Toulouse-Lautrec's
birthday (1864), which the evening piece borrows. The moon is in all five — in the sky, as a
date on a plate, as the surveyor's lamp, on a poster, and finally as itself.

## 1. Moonset over the Beaver Pond
Shin-hanga landscape, full palette with blue in charge. The moon going down over three
ridges of spruce while the horizon warms; lodge and dam mirrored in the pond, a beaver
towing its wake, a heron, and a freshly felled birch on the near bank with its pencil-point
stump. Morning, because this is what the pond looks like at six: the night shift ending.

## 2. The Lodge, in Section
Natural-history plate on white paper, numbered and keyed. The pond cut through: stick-and-mud
dome, the dry sleeping chamber with two occupants, plunge holes, the winter food raft, first
ice, trout, the dam, and one late worker bringing in aspen. Midday is for reading — the
brightest light, the most small type.

## 3. Beaver Brook
A survey sheet of an invented valley, green in charge. Generated terrain with contours, one
brook stepped into three ponds by three dams, lodges, canals dug out to the aspen, every
stump plotted, spruce on the heights, a footpath and a hide. Afternoon is the long view:
step back from the lodge and see what one family does to a landscape.

## 4. La Lune du Castor
Lithographic poster after Lautrec, red in charge: flat colour, black silhouette, and crachis
spatter instead of a regular screen. The supermoon rising through alders, ducks coming in,
and on the bank a beaver two-thirds through a trunk, rim-lit. Evening, because that is
moonrise, and when the work starts again.

## 5. Luna Plena
A page from a lunar atlas, mostly black. The full disc with its seas screened in blue,
craters and ray systems, a graduated limb, sixteen numbered features with a key, the
Pleiades, Uranus and Aldebaran in the margins, and the whole lunation as a strip of phases
with tonight ringed. Night is for looking up at the thing itself.

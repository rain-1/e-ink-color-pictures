# 2026-11-26 — wonderful things

Seed: on 26 November 1922, mid-afternoon, Howard Carter made a small breach in a sealed
doorway, held up a candle, and saw "a strange and wonderful medley of extraordinary and
beautiful objects heaped upon one another". Asked if he could see anything: "Yes, wonderful
things." Five readings of that day, all six inks, as much drawn detail as the panel will
hold. A red sun-disc travels through all five.

## 1. The Valley, Sunrise
Illustrated poster with pen hatching. El-Qurn against the rising sun, the cliff wall in lit
and shadowed buttresses, and below the tomb of Ramesses VI the cut with its sixteen steps,
the basket line, the camp, a donkey with water jars. Morning, because that is how the day
of days began — with people walking to work.

## 2. Khepri Pectoral
Cloisonné jewellery plate. A winged scarab holds the noon sun up on its forelegs and a shen
ring below; cobra frieze above, wedjat eyes and a lotus-and-poppy fringe below, stars set
into every leftover piece of lapis. Midday, because Khepri is the sun being pushed to the
top of the sky.

## 3. "Yes, Wonderful Things"
Candlelit tableau, Bayer-screened by distance from the flame. Through the ragged breach:
the lion, cow and Ammut couches, stacked food boxes, alabaster jars, chariot wheels, a
guardian statue; around it the plastered door with its jackal-and-nine-captives seals,
fading to black at the edges. Afternoon, because this is the hour it happened.

## 4. Garden Pool, Toward Evening
Theban tomb-painting manner: the pond in plan, fish, ducks and lotus in profile, date palms,
sycamore figs, doum palms and pomegranates laid flat on all four sides, flowers in every
gap. Above it the akhet sign — the sun going into the horizon — and half the lotuses have
already closed. Evening, for the garden the tomb owner hoped to sit in at the end of the day.

## 5. Nut, with Tonight's Sky
Astronomical ceiling. Nut arches over everything with stars in ranks on her body and the
twelve hours of night as red discs; the sun rides the night barque inside the serpent Mehen.
Under her arch is the real sky for tonight: Orion (Sah) rising on his side, Sirius (Sopdet)
behind him, Aldebaran above, and the waning gibbous moon two days after the Beaver Moon.
Night: mostly black, a little light in it.
